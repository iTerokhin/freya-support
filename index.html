FAQ

How do I use Freya?
Visit Home for daily inspiration and reflections. Open Cards to explore questions for self-reflection or conversations with friends. Find inspirational quote cards in Inspo, or go to Matches to create and view a Match Report.
Is Freya free?
Yes. The current features are free, with no subscription or purchase required.
Do I need an internet connection?
Daily texts, card browsing, and Match Report creation and viewing work on your device without an internet connection. City search, online pages, email, and sharing through other services may require a connection.
Where is my information stored?
Your profile and saved Match Reports are stored on your device. We do not operate a server-side user-profile database or provide our own cloud backup. We cannot remotely access or restore your local reports. See our Privacy Policy for details about storage, backups, and external services.
What is a Match Report?
A Match Report is a fun test that combines pre-written descriptions and relationship themes using the information you enter. Its scores are illustrative, not measurements of compatibility or verified facts about a person. Use it as a starting point for reflection and conversation.
Does Freya send my information to AI services?
No. Content is pre-written and selected on your device. The current App does not send the information you enter or the content of these features to AI services.
Can I suggest features?
Absolutely. Send your suggestions or feedback to iterokhin.dev@gmail.com. We would love to hear from you.
What should I include in a bug report?
Email iterokhin.dev@gmail.com with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Freya app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead. Screenshots can help; please hide any personal information you do not want to share.
Does Freya sell personal data?
No. We do not sell personal data. You can read more about how information is handled in our Privacy Policy.
