FAQ

How do I use Freya?
Freya works in a few simple steps: tell us about your goals, then get personalized daily life advice with clear, actionable tips tailored to your focus (confidence, career, self-growth). Need a quick boost? Tap Dopamine Cards for instant high-vibe motivation and inspiration.
Can I suggest features?
Absolutely — please send us any suggestions or comments you have. We'd love to hear from you at freya.app.help@gmail.com.
What happens if I find a bug?
Please contact us using the email above with a description of the problem and, if possible, screenshots. We'll do our best to fix it as quickly as possible.
Does Freya sell user data?
No. Freya does not sell people's data and will not sell anyone's data in the future.
